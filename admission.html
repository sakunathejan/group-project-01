Admission at ABC Academy

Learn how to apply, what to expect, and how ABC Academy supports every student on their learning journey.

Admission Overview

ABC Academy welcomes students from diverse backgrounds and offers a supportive environment to help each learner thrive academically and personally.

Application Process

  1. Review admission requirements and school programs.
  2. Complete the admission form and submit necessary documents.
  3. Attend a family visit or school tour.
  4. Receive admission decision and enrollment details.

Admission Requirements

  • Completed application form
  • Recent student report card or progress record
  • Proof of residency or guardian information
  • Optional parent/teacher recommendations

Enrollment Timeline

Applications are accepted year-round, with priority placement for students who apply early. We encourage families to begin the process as soon as possible.
